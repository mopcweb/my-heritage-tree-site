My Heritage Tree

Version 2.1.5 · 2026-09-28

A local-first desktop app for a family archive: draw each family as a tree, and keep everything about a person — facts, links, notes, labels and documents — in plain files on your own machine.

Download for macOSApple silicon · 19 MB Download for Windows64-bit · 32 MB

Installed once, the app keeps itself up to date: Settings → Updates.

The first time on a Mac: "Apple could not verify…"

Open the disk image, drag My Heritage Tree into Applications, and open it from there. macOS will say it could not verify the app and offer to move it to the Bin — press Done instead, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about My Heritage Tree, press Open Anyway, and confirm. Once is enough; updates need nothing.

The first time on Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

Press More info, then Run anyway. The installer puts the app away, adds a shortcut to the desktop and the Start menu, and shows where everything lives when it is done.
